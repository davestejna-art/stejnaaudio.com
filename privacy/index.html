Stejna Mix Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

In short: Stejna Mix collects no personal data. It talks to your mixer on your local network, and to Apple's App Store for the full mixer unlock. Nothing is sent to Stejna Audio.

What the app does with your network

Stejna Mix controls a Midas MR18 or Behringer XR18 / X18 digital mixer over Wi-Fi. To do that, it searches your local network for the mixer and exchanges control messages with it, such as fader levels, mutes, effect settings and meter readings. iPadOS asks your permission for this ("Local Network") the first time you open the app.

These messages travel only between your iPad and your mixer. The app has no servers, accounts or sign-in of its own.

The in-app purchase

The full mixer on your own mixer is a one-time in-app purchase. The app asks Apple's App Store for its price and whether your Apple Account owns it. Buying it, or tapping Restore purchase, is handled by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. Stejna Audio never receives your name, email address or payment details, and nothing about your mixer or how you use the app is sent. Apple reports sales to us only as totals.

What stays on your iPad

  • Preferences: your layout and display choices, perform groups, tap tempo, and the name and address of the mixer you last used. These are stored only on your iPad.
  • The unlock: a note that the full mixer is unlocked on this iPad, so it keeps working at a gig without internet.
  • Show files and presets that you save: these go wherever you choose in the Files app, such as on your iPad or in your iCloud Drive. If you choose iCloud Drive, Apple stores them under your Apple Account, as with any other file you save there.

Deleting the app removes its preferences from your iPad. Files you saved in the Files app stay where you put them until you delete them.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, usage tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party software development kits that collect data.
  • No collection of your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • No sharing or selling of data, because there is none to share.

Crash reports

If the app crashes and you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPad's settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share an anonymous crash report with us. We use these reports only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in those settings.

Children

Stejna Mix is a tool for operating audio equipment and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

stejnaaudio.com uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other sites.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
