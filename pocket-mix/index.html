Stejna Mix on iPhone

Your whole gig on your iPhone. Leave the iPad in the case: Pocket Mix puts big faders, every monitor mix and the channel controls you reach for at a show in your pocket.

Works with Midas MR18 and Behringer XR18 / X18. Requires iOS 16 or later.

Download on the App Store

Free to download. One purchase unlocks the full mixer on your iPhone and your iPad.

See it in action

Thirty seconds of Pocket Mix on an iPhone. The video has no sound.

Made for a phone on a mic stand

Your faders, big

ALL steps through every channel, eight at a time. Or keep up to four named groups of up to eight faders, like "Duo" or "Full band". Faders move from where they are, never jumping to your finger, and you can feel 0 dB click into place.

Every mix, one tap away

MAIN and the six monitor mixes sit across the top. On a mix's page, the same faders set what that player hears, and the master on the right is that mix's level.

The channel, at phone size

Tap a channel's name for gain and 48 V, an EQ you drag with your finger, the compressor, the gate and the effect sends. Tap the master for the main or monitor mix EQ.

A look inside

Tap a picture to see it full size.

For the whole band

Every player mixes their own monitors.

Each phone chooses once: the full mixer, or My Mix (personal monitoring). In My Mix a player's faders set only what they hear, and the main mix and everyone else's mixes are out of reach. Players can name and colour their own mix, so the engineer sees it at a glance. My Mix is free, so the whole band can install it. Set a house PIN, and the full mixer asks for it on every other phone and iPad.

A drummer with a stick in one hand? Pick ALL and use the big bank arrows. No setup at front of house.

Features

Faders

  • ALL or your own groups. Every channel, the Aux and the four effect returns, eight to a bank, or up to four named groups of up to eight. A stereo pair is one fader.
  • Each page keeps its own faders. MAIN shows your group; each monitor mix can show ALL or its own picks.
  • Never jumps. Touch anywhere on a fader's track and drag: it moves from where it is, at half your finger's speed. Grab the cap to move it one to one.
  • A click you can feel at 0 dB, and on every detent.
  • Mute groups as four big keys beside the master.

The channel page

  • Gain: the input meter with peak hold and clip, 48 V (it asks first), gain with -1 and +1 keys that land on whole decibels (and a click at 0 dB when you drag), low cut with clicks at the usual frequencies, and pan.
  • EQ: drag the bands on the curve; band keys, type and a 0 dB key; Q from narrow to wide.
  • Compressor: threshold over the live input and gain reduction, all twelve ratios as keys, makeup and auto.
  • Gate: threshold, range and all five modes.
  • Effects: the four sends, and each effect's own page with every control.
  • Mute group membership beside the channel's fader.

Mixes

  • MAIN and the six monitor mixes by name across the top. On a mix's page the faders are the sends into it.
  • The master's page: the main or monitor mix EQ, six bands, or the graphic EQ to ring out a wedge, plus its compressor.
  • Line check: every input's gain and meter on one screen.

The show

  • Snapshots with previous, next and GO.
  • Tap tempo at the top of the screen, flashing on the beat. Every delay follows it, and StompTap works from the phone too.
  • Scenes: load a show file from the Files app. You see what will change before anything is written, and it never turns on 48 V.
  • The same themes as the iPad, including Daylight for outdoor stages and Stage Red for a dark one.

One app, one purchase

Pocket Mix is Stejna Mix on iPhone, not a separate app. Install Stejna Mix, and your iPhone shows Pocket Mix while your iPad shows the full iPad mixer.

The demo mixer and My Mix (personal monitoring) are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks the full mixer on every iPhone and iPad with your Apple Account: buy it on one, then tap Restore purchase on the other. No subscription.
